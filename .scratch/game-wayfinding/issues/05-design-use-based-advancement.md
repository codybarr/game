# Design Use-Based Advancement

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Assignee: Cody
Blocked by: 04

## Question

What role should use-based advancement play alongside levels, loot, and build choice, and how will it reward authentic play without creating exploitable grinding or trapping players in accidental builds?

## Answer

Use a hybrid advancement model. Overall levels provide small fixed health and mana growth plus one player-allocated stat point; gear and specialization unlocks remain the dominant build levers. Stat points may be reassigned at the home region for a modest in-game cost.

Weapon and materia specialization mastery remains permanent and is earned from completed encounters and challenges, weighted by meaningful contribution such as damage, healing, or mitigation rather than raw action count. Any specialization can be started at any time and never requires a respec. An underdeveloped line receives accelerated credit until it reaches a baseline appropriate to the character's overall level, then advances at the normal rate. This makes experimentation viable while resisting idle and trivial-target grinding.
