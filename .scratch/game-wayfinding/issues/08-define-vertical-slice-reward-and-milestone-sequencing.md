# Define Vertical-Slice Reward and Milestone Sequencing

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Assignee: Cody

## Question

Across the home region and first authored expedition, where do common materials, at-risk valuables, named items, boss tokens, recipes, station milestones, major objectives, checkpoints, and the capstone boss appear—and in what sequence—so the vertical slice teaches its core loop, offers meaningful choices, and can be evaluated in a focused playtest?

## Answer

The 20–30 minute vertical-slice loop begins at home with the forge available. The player enters the authored introductory expedition and completes a short required combat encounter, earning common persistent materials and first basic loot. They then defeat an elite guardian, receiving a randomized item and a small at-risk valuable. A single checkpoint immediately afterward banks the valuable and becomes the expedition return point.

From that checkpoint, the player chooses or follows the hazardous side-on traversal route. Completing it grants an optional-but-obvious unique starter materia and an arcane-station recipe/material cache, then activates the sealed gate to the capstone boss. The boss grants a boss token and a choice among two or three curated named items. Returning home, the token unlocks the arcane station's deeper upgrade; the alchemy station is visible from the start and offers one basic consumable recipe. This demonstrates crafting, banking, exploration, combat, materia, boss progression, and post-expedition improvement in one focused playthrough.
