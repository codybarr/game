# Define Itemization, Stations, and Resource Economy

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Assignee: Cody

## Question

What item categories, stat and affix structure, home-station functions, and resource sinks should turn extracted expedition rewards into satisfying build optimization—without overwhelming the deliberately small vertical slice or making common materials, at-risk valuables, and completed-expedition farming redundant?

## Answer

The vertical slice supports weapon, armor, and one accessory slots. Equipment is a hybrid: randomized equipment has a base family that establishes its role and one or two affixes; curated named items are rare build-defining exceptions. The initial affix pool mixes readable stat bonuses—health, mana, attack, spell power, defense, and specialization-relevant stats—with a small set of playstyle modifiers such as meter gain, dodge efficiency, and elemental spell potency. Materia remains a separate, unique, non-randomized slottable system.

Home progression uses three narrow stations: a forge crafts and upgrades weapons and armor, an arcane station manages materia and mana-oriented upgrades, and an alchemy station makes limited expedition consumables. Drops provide surprising upgrades and named-item moments; common persistent materials let players craft or upgrade targeted bases. The slice intentionally excludes general affix rerolling.

Use common persistent materials for crafting and upgrades, rare boss tokens for station milestones or recipe unlocks, and at-risk currency/valuables for consumables and modest stat-point reassignment. This gives exploration, boss completion, and successful extraction distinct economic roles.
