# Define Combat and Build Identity

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01

## Question

What combat rhythm and build axes will make player choices mechanically rich, readable in the chosen 2D spatial grammar, and satisfying to optimize over many hours?

## Answer

Use side-on real-time action combat with free basic attacks and a universal dodge/dash; shield-capable weapons may add block/parry. Equipment mixes freely. Weapons and materia use advance permanent, independent specialization skill trees—such as Warrior, Archer, Black Mage, and White Mage—with no active job equipped. Unique, non-leveling materia supplies spells/buffs, while weapons unlock family-specific special attacks through specialization mastery. Weapon specials consume a meter built by normal attacks; materia spells consume mana.
