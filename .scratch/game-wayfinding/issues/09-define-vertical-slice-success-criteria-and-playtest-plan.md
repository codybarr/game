# Define Vertical-Slice Success Criteria and Playtest Plan

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Assignee: Cody

## Question

What is explicitly in and out of the 20–30 minute first-playthrough vertical slice, what player behaviors and outcomes will validate its fantasy and core loop, and how should focused playtests capture the evidence needed to decide what to refine next?

## Answer

The slice is limited to one home region, one authored introductory expedition, one weapon family, one starter materia, one elite guardian, one capstone boss, and the three limited home stations. It excludes additional weapon families and materia, second expeditions, expedition tiers, endgame, procedural assembly, and broad crafting.

The slice succeeds when its risky expedition/extraction loop and combat/build expression are both clear: a player understands what is at risk and why banking matters, and makes a meaningful weapon, materia, or loot choice that affects how they approach the boss. There will be no formal playtest program, telemetry, or structured interview. The practical bar is that Cody and friends can complete the slice without blockers and enjoy replaying it or voluntarily trying another named-item or materia choice.
