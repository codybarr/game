# Define Core Loop and Goal Cadence

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Assignee: Cody

## Question

What repeatable player loop should connect preparing at the home region, entering expedition zones, fighting and exploring, extracting or risking rewards, and unlocking the next objective—so that loot, use-based advancement, stations, bosses, and expedition tiers each create clear short-, medium-, and long-term goals?

## Answer

The player prepares at the home region, chooses an expedition, and explores toward a small set of telegraphed major objectives before challenging its capstone boss. Encounter-earned XP and specialization mastery, plus common materials, are permanent on acquisition. Rare gear, currency, and major rewards are at-risk valuables until the player banks them at a scarce expedition checkpoint or returns home. A banking checkpoint also becomes a persistent expedition return point.

After extraction, the player spends rewards at home stations to improve their build and pursues the expedition's objectives and boss. Major progression requires both station upgrades and boss victories. Completing a capstone boss unlocks the next expedition; the completed expedition remains farmable, and its harder tiers are optional long-term challenges for build optimization rather than gates on forward progress.
