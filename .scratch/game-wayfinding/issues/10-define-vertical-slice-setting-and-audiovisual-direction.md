# Define Vertical-Slice Setting and Audiovisual Direction

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Assignee: Cody

## Question

What minimal setting, narrative framing, visual direction, and audio tone should make the home region and introductory expedition feel coherent and memorable—while serving the gameplay-first, side-on RPG fantasy and remaining achievable for a solo Godot-first vertical slice?

## Answer

Set the game in a vast inhabited cavern. The home region is a fortified, lantern-lit camp, while expeditions descend into cold, shadowed, bioluminescent depths. The core visual language is crisp low-resolution pixel art: warm camp light contrasts with deep blue-green darkness, luminous crystals, and readable magical effects. Ancient machinery and ruins embedded in the cavern make recovered materia, treasure, and bosses feel natural.

The slice’s narrative is mostly environmental. The player is a lone delver expanding the camp into newly opened depths; no dialogue-heavy quest story is required. Audio emphasizes cave ambience—drips, distant rumbles, crystal or insect sounds, and footsteps—with sparse melodic camp music that intensifies in combat. This produces a coherent atmosphere without requiring extensive narrative or audio production.
