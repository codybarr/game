# Choose the World Authorship Model

Type: grilling
Status: resolved

## Question

How should hand-authored and procedural content divide responsibility in the game world, and what should each new playthrough or character experience differently?

## Answer

Use an authored home and a fixed authored introductory expedition for every new character. Later expeditions are procedurally assembled from hand-authored rooms, encounters, bosses, and rewards. A generated expedition persists when left voluntarily and, once completed, becomes a static farmable area with replenishing enemies and resources. Defeat abandons the active expedition and clears its temporary progress. Completed expeditions can be upgraded to harder tiers that retain their recognizable layouts while increasing enemy and reward tables.
